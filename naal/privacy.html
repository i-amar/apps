Privacy

Naal works out everything on your phone. It does not use the internet, and we receive nothing.

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no adverts and no tracking.
  • The app has no internet access. It sends nothing to us or to anyone else.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

What the app stores, and where

Naal keeps a few settings in its own storage on your phone so it opens the way you left it: your language, the town you chose, light or dark appearance, the last rasi you looked at, and whether you have seen the welcome screen. Test (beta) versions also keep the date they were first opened, so they know when the test period ends. None of this leaves your phone. If your phone's own backup is switched on (iCloud or Google), it may include these settings; that is between you and Apple or Google.

Your town

You choose your town from a list. The app does not use GPS or find your location by itself. The town is used only to work out sunrise, sunset and the day's times on your phone.

Copying a kural

If you tap Copy on the Kural of the day, the kural is placed on your phone's clipboard so you can paste it where you like. Nothing else is done with it.

Adverts

This version shows no adverts. If a future version ever adds them, this page will be updated first and will say what that means for your privacy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your phone removes everything it stored.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
