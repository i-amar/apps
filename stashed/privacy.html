STASHED

Privacy

Everything you put into Stashed stays on your iPhone. We receive nothing.

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no adverts and no tracking.
  • The app sends nothing over the internet. The only connection it ever makes is to Apple, for the Pro purchase.
  • We cannot see your rooms, places, items, notes or photos.

What the app stores, and where

The rooms, places, items, notes and photos you add are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If iCloud Backup is switched on for your iPhone, Apple may include the app's data in your device backup; that is between you and Apple.

Camera and photos

Stashed asks for the camera only when you take a photo of an item or scan a box label, and for your photo library only when you choose a picture from it. Photos are saved again inside the app without their location information. They never leave your iPhone unless you export a backup yourself.

Box labels

A printed QR label holds only a random code. It does not contain the box's name or what is inside, so nobody can read your belongings from the label.

Backups

You can export a backup file from Settings. It goes only where you choose to send or save it. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Purchases

Stashed Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, card or Apple Account details. Apple's own privacy policy covers that payment.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything in the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone does the same.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
